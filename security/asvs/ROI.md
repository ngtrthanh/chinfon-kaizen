# Compliance cost and ROI — planning assumptions, not quotation

Estimate using effort, not fictitious vendor pricing. Example working assumption: 2.5 million VND / engineer-day; 1 day = 8h.

| Package | Estimated effort | Cost estimate | Value driver |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| P0 authentication, authorization, HTTPS | 6–14 days | 15–35 M VND | Reduce likelihood of personal-data exposure |
| P1 upload, SAST, tests, CI | 4–10 days | 10–25 M VND | Repeatable verification, fewer regressions |
| Backups, monitoring, restore exercise | 4–8 days | 10–20 M VND | Reduce expected outage and data-loss cost |
| Independent penetration test + retest | External quotation required | Not estimated | Objective evidence, residual-risk discovery |

These are **illustrative planning ranges**; no actual billing data or vendor quotation was supplied.

Incremental ROI = (risk-adjusted expected losses avoided + measurable efficiency benefits - additional compliance cost) / additional compliance cost. Estimate baseline frequency and impact before assigning a monetary ROI. Mandatory legal / safety controls remain gates independent of ROI.
