## 1. Business outcome and bounded operating model

One official employee-recognition and innovation **case register** for a limited authorized workforce cohort. Each case is traceable from originator/evidence to independent review and explicit award decision. The system is not a live payroll processor and cannot assert payment from a button click.

**Prerequisite P0 data contracts**, even when hosted as separate applications:
- `PERSON-01`: active authorized employee, stable HR employee number, name, current department, status, valid dates.
- `ORG-03`: HR-approved reporting edge and explicitly authorized reviewer/scope as-of the case date, including acting/deputy by approved delegation. `report_to` by itself is **not** authorization.
- `TALENT-04`: Kaizen service owns initiative cases, evidence, review history, award decisions, and approved export. It consumes a **read-only approved snapshot** of the two upstream contexts during P0.

**Crucial boundary:** OrgAxis upstream production is not implemented: its `person.synthetic CHECK(synthetic=1)` and failing CI prohibit calling ORG-03/PERSON-01 live. A signed HR CSV bridge is a temporary verified input to Kaizen, NOT equivalent to shipping those OrgAxis modules. If the business strictly requires both standalone OrgAxis modules to be live first, the two-day GO-LIVE verdict is **NO-GO**.

## 3. Frozen RBAC / decision rights (proposal, must receive written business signoff)

| Actor | Allowed P0 operations | Explicitly disallowed |
|---|---|---|
| WORKER | create, edit draft, submit, view own/co-authored case, answer NEED_INFO | approve own or another case; broad department visibility |
| FOREMAN/SHIFT LEADER | field verification **only when assigned an authorized SUPERVISOR permission and date/scope** | automatic permission from foreman title or shift presence |
| MANAGER | stage review for approved department; comment/reject/request evidence | cross-department review without named delegation; own proposal approval |
| REVIEWER / COUNCIL | independently assess benefit and propose level; final decision only by authorized named actor | same person self-review/self-approve; exceptional override without evidence |
| DIRECTOR/CEO designated | formal final approval within documented monetary limits | automatically approve own case; unrestricted payment edit |
| HRD/FINANCE | staff/role reconciliation, controlled export, payment record verification, audit | invent reward decision or mark as paid without external evidence |
| ADMIN (IT) | manage technical account, deployment and backup | business award approval merely by being ADMIN; read sensitive case contents without separate authorization |

Role and effective scope must be captured at **decision time**. Revocation/termination, transfer and substitution must close previous grants and sessions. Importing a CSV must not silently re-enable departed staff or elevate role privileges. No `dept_scope=ALL` from job title alone.
